title: "Perfect Days, Knotted"
author: "Anbu"
categories: poetry
---

**Japanese brother and German sister—**
they converse deep, as they usually do.
  
Now that the sister is sick,
with induced levels of cortisol
and a lack of control,

  **“You should slow down,”**
advises the brother.
  
Inspiration, from all directions, abounds:
WhatsApp channels anonymous,
many on mute—
one mentions _Perfect Days_,
directed by a German,
played by a Japanese.

A film of one content with life,
finding joy in the little,
a watch for the hospital stay.

How can coincidences
be so multi-knotted—
in time, in geography,
in substance?

Personal growth, it seems,
reflects even
on the bizarre levels
of coincidence.
